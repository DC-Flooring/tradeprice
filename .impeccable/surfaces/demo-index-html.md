# Home (demo/index.html)

Scope: the logged-out home page. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and action: new North East fitters sign up for a free trade account (primary). Existing customers sign in (secondary). Proof on hand: sample prices from demo data only; testimonial, address, hours, phone and counter photo are placeholders.

Constraints: brand pinned by the user (black #0B0A09, DC gold #B8924A, Singel headlines, Auttera accents used sparingly, Barlow numbers, Inter body). Approved composition: design/Home.dc.html. User asked to avoid boxes around everything and repetitive section layouts. No eyebrow labels.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the counter in your pocket: it opens on your own price list, not a promise about one. It refuses the stacked card-grid landing page where every section is a rounded box.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black van-cab ground, DC gold used as tape-measure rulings and one solid band, warm off-white for the voucher. Singel display, Auttera hand-written in one accent per screen, Barlow tabular prices. Hairline rules instead of boxes; the only framed objects are things that are objects in life: the price screen and the voucher.

STORY: See your price, stock and cut status at a glance; believe signing up is quick and local; tap Sign up free. Regulars tap Sign in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo left, Sign in pill right. "Trade prices." in Singel at full width with "In your pocket." in Auttera gold below. One-line lede, a full-width gold Sign up free button, three ticks. The price screen starts in view below on a phone, beside the copy on desktop, with the CUT today sticker stamping on.

FORM: Pinned by the user: the approved home design (design/Home.dc.html), unboxed. seed: none (direction pinned by user)
