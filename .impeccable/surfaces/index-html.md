# Home (index.html)

Scope: the logged-out home page. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and action: new North East fitters sign up for a free trade account (primary). Existing customers sign in (secondary). Proof on hand: example prices only, plus two real photos (kicker, tea). Testimonial, address, hours, phone and counter photo are placeholders.

Constraints: brand pinned in PRODUCT.md. Approved composition: design/Home.dc.html, unboxed. No eyebrow labels. User copy is binding. Sign up and sign in link to apply.html and login.html, which do not exist yet by the user's choice.

Deferred, by decision: the three price-preview swatches stay CSS stand-ins until real product swatch photos arrive. Nothing new is generated for them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the counter in your pocket: it opens on your own price list, not a promise about one. It refuses the stacked card-grid landing page where every section is a rounded box.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black van-cab ground, DC gold as tape-measure rulings and one solid closing band, warm paper for the voucher and sticker. Barlow Condensed 700 capitals for headlines, Barlow for prices, Inter for body, Auttera for pencil-style marks (the hero script, the price-screen notes, dimension lines, the voucher stub, the closer script). Hairline rules instead of boxes; the only framed objects are things that are objects in life: the price screen, the voucher and the photos. Real trade photos, warm graded, rounded inline or full-bleed with fades.

STORY: See your price, stock and cut status at a glance; believe signing up is quick and local; tap Sign up free. Regulars tap Sign in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky see-through header with logo and Sign in. "TRADE PRICES." in Barlow Condensed at full width with "In your pocket." in Auttera gold below. One-line lede, a full-width gold Sign up free button, and "2 minutes · No paperwork · Free" with gold dot separators. The price screen starts in view below on a phone (beside the copy on desktop), with pencil notes above it, the Cut today sticker and a 5.2 m dimension line under it.

FORM: Pinned by the user: the approved home design (design/Home.dc.html), unboxed, then extended by the user's personality and photo briefs. seed: none (direction pinned by user; no roll)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished.
